## Welcome to the intersection of biology and computation

Modern biology generates more data than ever. **bioinfo.guru helps life science learners turn that data into discovery** through approachable teaching, guided practice, and research-focused computational tools.

Whether you are taking your first steps with Python or building a reproducible genomics workflow, find a path that fits your goals.

| 🎓 Learn | 🧪 Practice | 🚀 Apply |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Self-paced courses and clear learning resources | Guided workshops, interactive tools, and games | Mentored internships and research support |

## Find your learning path

### 🧑‍🏫 Hands-on workshops

Build practical skills through focused, instructor-led training. Workshops are designed for life science students, PhD scholars, faculty, clinicians, and research professionals. Work through guided analyses and take home methods you can use with your own research data.

**Workshop themes include:**

- **Bioinformatics with R** — analyze and visualize biological data
- **Data science for biologists** — bring data-driven methods into life-science research
- **Molecular docking** — explore structure-based approaches to drug discovery
- **AI for biological sciences** — discover applications of AI and language models in biology
- **Python web apps** — build useful computational skills and applications
- And many more...

👉 [Browse workshops and registration information](https://bioinfo.guru/workshops/)

### 🔬 Personalised research internships

Get dedicated one-to-one online mentoring and learn at a pace that works for you. Choose a focused **one-month** programme to build a specific skill, or a **five-month** programme to develop research experience through a tailored project. The longer programme can also support dissertation or thesis work.

Sessions are online, one-to-one, and scheduled with your instructor. [Explore internship details](https://bioinfo.guru/internships/)

### 💻 Learn online, at your own pace

Browse self-paced courses in bioinformatics and computational biology through our **Dig**ital **Le**arning portal ([DigLe](https://digle.bioinfo.guru)). Start with a concept, build a skill, and revisit lessons when your research calls for them.

## A toolkit for curious researchers

The [futures resources](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/) brings together learning materials, research utilities, and playful ways to practise.

| Resource shelf | What you’ll find |
|---|---|
| 📚 [Ebooks](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/ebooks/) | Learning guides for Python, R, programming, data analysis, genomics, and molecular biology. Try the [Python book](https://pythonbook.bioinfo.guru) and [R book](https://rbook.bioinfo.guru). |
| 🧬 [Alignment tools](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/alignment-tools/) | Sequence alignment utilities, including [MSA Annotator](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/alignment-tools/msa-annotator/) and [SS-DIA](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/alignment-tools/ss-dia/). |
| 🧪 [Docking tools](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/docking-tools/) | Molecular docking and interaction analysis resources, including [PLIVA](https://pliva.bioinfo.guru) and a [molecular docking project on GitHub](https://github.com/bioinfoguru/molecular_docking). |
| 👩‍💻 [Coding tools](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/coding/) | [Kalonji](https://kalonji.bioinfo.guru), a desktop notebook IDE; [Pycloud](https://pycloud.bioinfo.guru) for Python app hosting; and an [AI coding tutor](https://aitutor.bioinfo.guru) for Python and R. |
| 🧰 [Utilities](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/utilities/) | Research and productivity helpers, including [SPADE](https://spade.bioinfo.guru), a cross-platform PDF editor. |
| 🎮 [Learning games](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/games/) | Play [Docking Rush](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/games/docking-rush/), [Biology Hangman](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/games/biology-hangman/), [The Codon Game](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/games/codon-game/), [The Amino Acid Game](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/games/amino-acid-game/), and [Immune System Defense](https://bioinfo.guru/resources/games/immune-defense/). |
